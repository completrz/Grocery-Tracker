CartTrail turns grocery receipts into a spending history. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain. If anything is unclear, contact us at {{CONTACT}}.

Who we are

CartTrail is run by {{OPERATOR}} (“we”). You can reach us at {{CONTACT}}.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address and password. Your password is stored by our sign-in provider as a one-way hash, so we never see it. If you add a display name or a profile picture, we keep those too. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email address and basic profile from Google.
  • The receipts you add. The store name, purchase date, item names, quantities and prices, and the categories you or the app assign to items. Totals and trends are calculated from these.
  • Receipt photos and PDFs. When you scan a file, it is sent to Microsoft Azure to be read. CartTrail does not keep the file afterwards; only the items you choose to save are stored.
  • Store locations. If a receipt shows a store address, it can be sent to the U.S. Census Geocoder to find the store’s approximate position. If you confirm a store branch or pin one on the map, we save the branch name, address and coordinates. We never collect your device’s location.
  • Your settings. Your monthly budget, notification choices and time zone.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications, we store the address and keys your browser’s push service gives us for your device, and a log of which notifications we sent, so none is sent twice.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the service. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don’t read your receipts for any purpose other than running CartTrail for you.

How we use it

To read your receipts, save them to your account, show your spending, trends, price changes and store comparisons, send the notifications you ask for, and keep the service secure and working.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it. You can delete your receipts and data, or your whole account, at any time from your Profile (see how to delete your data). Deleting your account removes your data from our database and file storage straight away. Our providers may keep encrypted backups for a limited time afterwards.

Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your receipts and items in the app.
  • Download everything as a CSV file from your Profile.
  • Delete your data or your account from your Profile.
  • Turn notifications on or off, or choose which kinds you get, from your Profile.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Contact us at {{CONTACT}} and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and every account’s data is protected by rules that stop other accounts from reading it. No service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

CartTrail is not meant for children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets a higher age). We don’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
