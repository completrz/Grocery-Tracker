Delete your data

You can delete your CartTrail data, or your whole account, yourself at any time.

In the app

  1. Sign in to CartTrail.
  2. Open your Profile (the circle with your initial or picture, top right).
  3. Go to Your data.
  4. Choose one of:
    • Delete my receipts and data. Removes your receipts, items, saved store branches, remembered categories and budget. Your account stays so you can start again.
    • Delete my account. Removes all of the above, plus your profile, picture, notification settings and devices, and then deletes your sign-in.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm (and enter your password if you sign in with email).

Deleting cannot be undone. If you might want your history later, choose Export CSV first.

What gets deleted

Everything listed above is removed from our database and file storage straight away. Our providers may keep encrypted backups for a limited time afterwards.

If you can’t sign in

Email {{CONTACT}} from the address on your account, tell us you want your data or account deleted, and we will do it within 30 days of confirming the request is from you.
