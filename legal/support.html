Quick answers

How do I add a receipt?

Choose Scan a receipt, then take a photo, pick a file, drop one onto the page, or paste a screenshot. JPEG, PNG and PDF files up to 4 MB work. On Android, you can also share a screenshot or PDF to CartTrail from Photos, Gmail or Files once the app is installed. Check the items, then save.

The scanner couldn’t read my receipt.

Try a sharper photo in good light, with the whole receipt flat and in frame. A clear PDF works well too. You can always add or fix lines by hand before saving.

The subtotal doesn’t match.

The scanner may have misread a price or missed a line. Compare each line with your receipt and fix the one that’s off. If the receipt doesn’t print a separate subtotal, CartTrail uses its total, and you can correct the amount in the “Printed subtotal” box.

How do I install the app?

On Android or a computer, open your Profile and tap Install CartTrail. On iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Open your Profile and find Notifications. You can choose which kinds you get, or turn them off for that device. On iPhone, notifications only work once CartTrail is on your home screen.

How do I download my data?

Open your Profile, then Your data, and choose Export CSV.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page, enter your email and choose Forgot your password?. We will email you a link. If you signed in with Google, use “Sign in with Google” instead.
